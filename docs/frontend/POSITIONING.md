## 📐 Page-by-Page Blueprint

### `/` (Home)
- Hero: Animated data visualization + succinct pitch.
- Social proof: Logos/testimonials (if available).
- Snapshot metrics: AI queries answered, training improvements, uptime.
- CTA: “View architecture” (link to `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` summary) and “Let’s build together”.

### `/projects`
- Cards for flagship projects (RAG engine, trainer agent, analytics dashboards).
- Each card: Problem → Solution → Impact → Tech stack badges → “Read documentation”.
- Embed interactive demos or video walkthroughs.

### `/whoop-dashboard`
- Live data exploration (default to last 7 days).
- AI assistant entry point (inline chat for questions like “What trend should I focus on?”).
- Compare metrics vs historical averages.

### `/ai-lab` (optional)
- Showcase experiments (prompt engineering, evaluation results).
- Provide callouts to `docs/ai/*.md` for readers to dive deeper.

### `/about`
- Combine professional narrative with personal story (health, philosophy, values).
- Timeline referencing `docs/knowledge/PROJECT_HISTORY.md`.
- Link to `docs/knowledge/CAMILO_PROFILE.md` for detailed profile.

## 🎞️ Interaction Guidelines

- **Scroll Storytelling**: Progressive animation reveals metrics and context; avoid gratuitous motion.
- **Data Hover States**: Provide tooltips with definitions (source `docs/knowledge/GLOSSARY.md`).
- **AI Interactions**: Always include the SQL snippet and rationale; highlight safety and explainability.
- **Responsive Breakpoints**: Maintain parity across mobile, tablet, desktop; hero sections must remain legible.
- **Performance**: Aim for 60fps animations; degrade gracefully on low-power devices.

## 📣 Content Voice & Tone

| Dimension | Guideline | Example |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Voice** | Confident, precise, generous with knowledge | “Here’s the architecture powering real-time insights.” |
| **Tone** | Inspirational yet grounded in metrics | “Recovered 92% faster after optimizing sleep schedule.” |
| **Style** | Blend storytelling with analytics | Combine narrative paragraphs with charts and callouts |
| **CTA Language** | Outcome-driven | “Explore the AI pipeline” instead of “Learn more” |

### Copy Checklist
- Lead with outcomes, support with data.
- Use active voice (“Built”, “Delivered”, “Measured”).
- Avoid jargon unless defined in glossary.
- Always tie technical complexity to business or personal impact.
