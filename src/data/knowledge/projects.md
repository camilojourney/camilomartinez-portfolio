# Projects

## Holus — AI Marketing Strategist
Status: In Progress
Description: Autonomous content engine with 32 specialized agents. Observes analytics, reasons about strategy using Claude Opus, creates content via specialized agents (hook writers, blog writers, carousel architects, SEO researchers), and evaluates every piece with 7 domain-expert quality judges. Promotes Pilaster, Genpeli, and Invoz across 5 platforms.

## Holus Observatory — Multi-Agent Monitoring Dashboard
Status: In Progress
